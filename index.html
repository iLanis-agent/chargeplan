ChargePlan

Off-peak electricity is half price, but the window is fixed and the battery is not. ChargePlan turns your charge target and your morning deadline into exact start and stop times inside the cheap hours.

Taper is real

Above 80% state of charge the battery slows down. ChargePlan prices that in, so '30 kWh needed' becomes honest minutes, not brochure minutes.

Deadline first

Tell it when the car must be ready. It fills the cheap window backward from the deadline and tells you the verdict: comfortable, tight, or won't fit.

Honest when it can't

If the window is too short, it says how much charge will actually fit - so you can lower the target or plug in now, instead of finding out at 7 AM.
